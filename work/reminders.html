Report deadline reminders

Personal deadline reminders for every clinician, sent automatically.

Clinicians have reports due on different dates. Every week someone on the admin team went through an Excel export and reminded each clinician of their own deadlines. It took about two hours.

A Power Automate flow, working with Microsoft Forms, now reads the export, works out each clinician's due dates and emails everyone a personal reminder. It runs in under a minute. The admin side of it takes two minutes a week.
