Patient registry

Tracks patients across clinical programs and keeps their progress dashboard up to date.

The registry follows patients across clinical programs. It sends outcome survey reminders on its own, and the progress dashboard refreshes every day.

It is a relational data model in Dataverse with a model-driven Power Apps interface, Power Automate flows for the reminders and Power BI for the dashboard.
