Generative AI

What I build

  • AI agents and multi-agent systems
  • Document-processing pipelines for summarization, classification and extraction
  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) applications that ground answers in your own data
  • Copilot Studio agents for Microsoft 365
  • MCP servers and custom APIs to integrate LLMs with your systems
  • LLM selection, evaluation and cost estimates

How I work

Most of my AI work has been in healthcare, so privacy and accuracy come first in every project.

  • Your privacy and data residency rules decide which models and platforms we use.
  • Every app and agent only reaches the data and tools it needs.
  • New models and frameworks are tested against your evals before they replace anything.

I build two ways.

  • Custom LLM apps and agents in Python, with LangChain, LangGraph, Pydantic AI or the OpenAI Agents SDK. Best when you need your own logic and interface, any model, and full control over hosting and cost.
  • Copilot Studio agents, for deep integration with Microsoft 365 and the Power Platform through topics, Power Automate flows, connectors, MCP servers and internal knowledge sources, with Entra ID managing access.

How a project runs

  1. Requirements and success criteria.
  2. Data sources, quality and compliance.
  3. RAG pipeline, when the model needs to answer from your own data.
  4. Business rules, guardrails and human-in-the-loop approvals.
  5. Architecture, with agents, tools, handoffs and structured outputs.
  6. Model selection by accuracy, latency and cost.
  7. Async FastAPI backend with Pydantic validation and MCP servers.
  8. Evals with real questions and expected answers, run before every release.
  9. Interface, such as a web chat, Microsoft Teams or a REST API.
  10. Deployment with Docker and CI/CD to Azure or the cloud you already use.
  11. Observability, with tracing, latency, token usage and cost.
