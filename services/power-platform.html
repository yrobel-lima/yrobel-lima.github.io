Power Platform

Flows and apps that remove the copying, retyping and chasing from your team's week.

What I build

  • Workflow automation with Power Automate
  • Canvas and model-driven apps
  • Dataverse data models
  • Document processing with AI Builder
  • Custom connectors to your APIs

How a project runs

  1. Current process, who does each step and where time is lost.
  2. Business rules and exceptions.
  3. Data sources, permissions, and the Dataverse model if new tables are needed.
  4. Triggers, connectors and flows, within your data loss prevention policies.
  5. Canvas or model-driven apps, if people need screens to take part.
  6. Error handling in every flow, with an alert when a run fails.
  7. Testing with real cases.
  8. Development in an unmanaged solution with the tables, apps, flows, connection references and environment variables.
  9. Deployment with Power Platform pipelines as a managed solution to test and production managed environments.
