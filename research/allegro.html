ALLEGRO core analysis

I built a 3D heterogeneous reference model of the ALLEGRO gas-cooled fast reactor core in Serpent (Monte Carlo) and deterministic models in ERANOS 2.3.

I ran criticality, reactor safety and burnup calculations over a full year of operation.

I benchmarked the ERANOS models against the Serpent reference to find calculation options fast enough for optimization. The best setup cut calculation time with little loss in accuracy.
