ALLEGRO models

3D neutronics models of the ALLEGRO gas-cooled fast reactor core.

I built a 3D heterogeneous reference model of the ALLEGRO core in Serpent (Monte Carlo) and deterministic models in ERANOS 2.3.

At beginning of life I calculated k-eff, flux and power distributions, the Doppler constant, the effect of helium density on reactivity and beta-eff. I also followed k-eff and fuel isotope masses over 365 days of operation, with the breeding ratio and average burnup.
