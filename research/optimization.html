Fuel loading optimization

I developed metaheuristic optimization code in Python, coupled to ERANOS 2.3, to find the best fuel loading pattern for the ALLEGRO core.

I implemented genetic algorithms and tabu search, and wrote a new ERANOS procedure to swap fuel assemblies.

The best arrangements improved fuel cycle performance by 15%.
