Thorium fuel study

I built a 3D Monte Carlo model of the GFR2400 gas-cooled fast reactor core in Serpent to study thorium as a replacement for uranium.

The thorium-plutonium fuel could extend the operating cycle while keeping the main core parameters close to the uranium-plutonium reference.
