Thorium in GFR2400

Swapping uranium for thorium in a large fast reactor.

I built a detailed Serpent model of the GFR2400 and compared two fuels, (U,Pu)C and (Th,Pu)C. I looked at k-eff, the neutron spectrum, flux, the Doppler constant, the depressurization effect, control rod worth and kinetics.

Over 1450 effective full power days, the uranium-233 bred in the thorium core can extend the cycle while the steady state parameters stay similar.
