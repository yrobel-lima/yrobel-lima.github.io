About me

I'm an AI engineer based in Austin, Texas. Since 2024 I've built LLM applications and automations for Tall Tree Health, a healthcare provider in Victoria, British Columbia, including:

  • REST APIs with FastAPI
  • Multi-agent apps with the OpenAI Agents SDK
  • Copilot Studio agents in Microsoft Teams
  • MCP servers
  • Web apps with Python and Power Apps
  • RAG pipelines

I spent six years doing reactor physics research. I have a master's and a Ph.D. in engineering from UNAM in Mexico City, both in nuclear reactor design and analysis. I built 3D full-core models with the Serpent and ERANOS neutron transport codes and ran burnup and safety calculations. I processed and analyzed the simulation data in Python and MATLAB. For my Ph.D., I wrote metaheuristic optimization code in Python for in-core fuel management. During that time I published four journal articles and two conference papers.

I also worked as a medical physicist in Cuba, planning radiotherapy treatments for more than 500 patients.
