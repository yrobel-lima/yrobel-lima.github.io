About me

I'm an AI and automation developer in Austin, Texas.

I build LLM applications and automations for Tall Tree Health, an integrated healthcare provider in Victoria, British Columbia.

  • REST APIs with FastAPI
  • Multi-agent apps with the OpenAI Agents SDK
  • Copilot Studio agents in Microsoft Teams
  • MCP servers
  • Web apps with Python and Power Apps
  • RAG pipelines

I have a master's and a Ph.D. in engineering from UNAM in Mexico City. My master's thesis studied the use of thorium in gas-cooled fast reactors. My Ph.D. thesis, Fuel loading pattern optimization in the ALLEGRO gas-cooled fast reactor through metaheuristics, coupled Python optimizers built on genetic algorithms and tabu search to the ERANOS code. The research is published in four journal articles and two conference papers.

I also worked as a medical physicist at the Provincial Oncology Hospital in Villa Clara, Cuba, where I planned radiotherapy treatments for more than 500 patients.
